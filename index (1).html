2. Lead-in: what is happening here? 5 min

Coffee break at the Port Services Expo

Marco: Hi, I don't think we've met. I'm Marco, from Delta Marine Services.

Dana: Nice to meet you, Marco. I'm Dana. I'm a services buyer at a metalworking company.

Marco: Oh, interesting! How long have you been working in the port area?

Dana: About six years. We work with many repair providers. What does Delta Marine do exactly?

Marco: We repair cranes and heavy equipment. What's the hardest part of your job?

Dana: Meeting deadlines when a supplier is late with a revised quotation.

Marco: I get that. We send quotations within 48 hours. Could I send you our company profile?

Dana: That would be great. Here's my card. It was really nice talking to you!

Marco: Likewise. I'll send you an email next week.

4. Natural or not? 4 min

AvoidBetter
“Good morning. I am Jessica. I am a buyer.”“Hi, I'm Jessica. I work in purchasing at a metalworking company. And you?”
“Do you have many clients?”“What kind of clients do you work with?”
“Give me your best price.”“What kind of projects are you working on these days?”
“OK. Bye.”“I don't want to keep you. It was great meeting you. I'll email you.”
“Hello. Send your catalog.”“Hi Marco, it was nice meeting you at the expo. Could you send me your company profile?”

Dica: você não precisa de frases perfeitas. Curiosidade e atenção valem mais. Explicar uma palavra com outras palavras também é uma habilidade.

5. Build your own sentences 6 min

a) Make it natural

“Good afternoon. I am Jessica. I am a buyer of services. I work in the company X.”

b) Closed → open question

Do you like your job?

Do you work with ports?

Is your company big?

c) Choose a follow-up

The supplier says: “We repair cranes, mostly for ports.” Write the best follow-up question and tell why it works.

d) Complete the conversation

Jessica: “What does your company ?”

“We provide welding and pipe repair.”

Jessica: “Really? And is your typical for a repair?”

“Usually two weeks.”

Jessica: “Good to know. you send me your later?”

Someone does not know what a services buyer does. Start with “Basically, I…”

7. Roleplay: a port industry event 9 min

Your card. You are Jessica, a services buyer, at a networking event at the port. You meet a new person next to the coffee.
Goal: start the conversation, learn about the person's company, explain your work, ask at least 4 follow-up questions, exchange contacts and agree on a follow-up. Do not negotiate yet.

8. Reflection 3 min

Homework

About 40–50 minutes. Your audio counts as your next recording (every 15 days) toward your goal for July 27, 2028.

Part 1 · Professional introduction (5–7 lines)

Include your job, your responsibilities and who you work with. Use at least 4 words from the last lesson.

Part 2 · At least 8 open questions

Start with What, How, Why, Which, Where or Who. Use Could/Would in at least 2.

Part 3 · Conversation (10–12 exchanges)

Greeting, small talk, jobs, follow-up questions, closing.

Part 4 · Recording (1–2 minutes)

Introduce yourself and explain how you would start a conversation with a new supplier. Use keywords only. Do not read. If you make a mistake, keep going.
